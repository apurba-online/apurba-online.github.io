Featured work

RA-CMF: Region-Adaptive Conditional MeanFlow for CT Image Reconstruction

RA-CMF combines a conditional MeanFlow reconstruction backbone with region-adaptive refinement to focus additional modeling effort on spatial regions that remain difficult after global reconstruction. The broader goal is reliable CT reconstruction that preserves clinically meaningful anatomy, texture, and quantitative imaging information.

About

I am a PhD Candidate at UAB working at the intersection of machine learning, medical image analysis, and biomedical data science. My research emphasizes methods that improve reconstruction and learned representations while preserving clinically meaningful information.

Before starting my doctoral research, I worked in software engineering and enterprise technology roles. That engineering background continues to shape how I approach reproducibility, implementation, and practical biomedical AI systems.
