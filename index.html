Featured Research · ICIBM 2026 Oral

RA-CMF: Region-Adaptive Conditional MeanFlow for CT Image Reconstruction

RA-CMF combines a conditional MeanFlow reconstruction backbone with region-adaptive refinement so that additional modeling effort is concentrated on spatial regions that are more difficult to reconstruct.

The project is motivated by a practical biomedical imaging problem: improving reconstruction consistency without indiscriminately modifying the entire CT image or sacrificing clinically relevant local information.

About

Biomedical informatics with a computer science foundation.

I am a PhD Candidate in Biomedical Health Informatics and Data Science at UAB. My research centers on machine learning systems for medical imaging and downstream biomedical analysis.

I am particularly interested in generative models that can improve CT reconstruction while preserving the information clinicians and quantitative imaging pipelines rely on. Beyond image reconstruction, I work on representation learning and predictive modeling for biomedical applications.

Before beginning my doctoral research, I worked in software engineering and enterprise IT in Bangladesh. That engineering background continues to shape how I approach research: methods should be technically rigorous, reproducible, and useful in realistic settings.
