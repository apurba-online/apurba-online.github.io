About

I am pursuing a PhD in Biomedical Health Informatics and Data Science at UAB. My research sits at the intersection of machine learning, medical image analysis, and biomedical data science, with an emphasis on methods that improve image reconstruction while preserving clinically meaningful anatomy, texture, and quantitative imaging information.

Before starting my doctoral research, I worked in software engineering and enterprise technology roles in Bangladesh. That engineering background continues to shape my research approach: I am interested in methods that are technically rigorous, reproducible, and useful in realistic biomedical settings.

Research Interests

  • Generative AI for medical imaging
  • CT image reconstruction
  • Flow matching and MeanFlow models
  • Region-adaptive and reinforcement learning methods
  • Biomedical representation learning
  • Radiomics and quantitative imaging
  • Multimodal biomedical AI
  • Predictive modeling for clinical outcomes
