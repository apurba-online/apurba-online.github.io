About

I am a PhD Candidate in Biomedical Health Informatics and Data Science at the University of Alabama at Birmingham. My research focuses on reliable machine learning systems for medical imaging and downstream biomedical analysis.

I am particularly interested in generative models that improve CT reconstruction while preserving clinically meaningful anatomy, texture, and quantitative imaging information. My broader work includes representation learning, predictive modeling, multimodal biomedical AI, and radiomics.

Before beginning my doctoral research, I worked in software engineering and enterprise IT in Bangladesh. That engineering background continues to shape how I approach research: methods should be technically rigorous, reproducible, and useful in realistic settings.

Research

FEATURED RESEARCH · ICIBM 2026 ORAL

RA-CMF: Region-Adaptive Conditional MeanFlow for CT Image Reconstruction

RA-CMF combines a conditional MeanFlow reconstruction backbone with region-adaptive refinement so that additional modeling effort is concentrated on spatial regions that are more difficult to reconstruct.

The work emphasizes reconstruction fidelity together with preservation of local anatomy, texture, and radiomic consistency.
