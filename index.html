About

Research at the intersection of AI and biomedical imaging.

My academic work focuses on building reliable learning systems for medical imaging while preserving clinically meaningful anatomy, texture, and quantitative imaging information.

I am a PhD Candidate in Biomedical Health Informatics and Data Science at UAB. My current research investigates generative AI methods for CT reconstruction and standardization, including conditional flow-based models and spatially adaptive refinement strategies.

I am especially interested in methods that connect image generation with downstream biomedical utility: representation learning, radiomics, multimodal prediction, and clinically meaningful evaluation. Alongside research, I bring prior industry experience in software engineering, enterprise systems, automation, and telecommunications.

I welcome research collaborations and opportunities involving generative AI, medical imaging, biomedical informatics, machine learning, and applied data science.

Research

Current research directions

I work on learning systems that reconstruct, represent, and analyze biomedical images while emphasizing structural and quantitative fidelity.

01 · GENERATIVE IMAGING

CT Reconstruction & Standardization

Conditional generative models for reducing protocol- and reconstruction-related variability in CT while preserving anatomy, texture, and radiomic information.

02 · ADAPTIVE LEARNING

Region-Adaptive Refinement

Spatially selective learning and reinforcement-learning policies that identify difficult regions, allocate refinement effort, and avoid unnecessary modification.

03 · REPRESENTATION

Biomedical Representation & Prediction

Image representations for downstream clinical modeling, including multimodal fusion, predictive models, radiomics, and vision-language learning.
