Current research focus

My work centers on reliable AI methods that reconstruct, represent, and analyze biomedical images while preserving clinically meaningful structure and quantitative information.

Featured workICIBM 2026 OralTravel Award

RA-CMF: Region-Adaptive Conditional MeanFlow for CT Image Reconstruction

RA-CMF combines a conditional MeanFlow reconstruction backbone with region-adaptive refinement so additional modeling effort is concentrated on spatial regions that are more difficult to reconstruct. The framework is designed to improve reconstruction consistency while preserving anatomy, subtle texture, and radiomic information.

About

Building clinically meaningful AI systems for biomedical imaging.

I am a PhD Candidate in Biomedical Health Informatics and Data Science at UAB. My research centers on machine learning systems for medical imaging and downstream biomedical analysis, with particular interest in generative models that improve CT reconstruction while preserving information clinicians and quantitative imaging pipelines rely on.

Before beginning my doctoral research, I worked in software engineering and enterprise IT in Bangladesh. That engineering background continues to shape how I approach research: methods should be technically rigorous, reproducible, and useful in realistic settings.
