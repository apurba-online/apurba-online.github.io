Featured research

RA-CMF: Region-Adaptive Conditional MeanFlow for CT Image Reconstruction

ICIBM 2026 · Regular oral presentation · arXiv ↗

RA-CMF combines a Conditional MeanFlow backbone for global CT reconstruction with region-adaptive refinement. The framework is designed to focus additional modeling effort on spatial regions that remain difficult after the global reconstruction stage.

The central goal is reliable reconstruction that preserves clinically relevant local anatomy, texture, and quantitative image characteristics rather than applying indiscriminate corrections across the image.

High-level pipeline: source CT → Conditional MeanFlow reconstruction → regional assessment → selective refinement → reconstructed CT.

Evaluation focus

  • Full-image reconstruction quality using PSNR and SSIM.
  • Tumor-region reconstruction quality.
  • Radiomic feature consistency across first-order and texture families.
  • Preservation of clinically relevant structure and texture.
