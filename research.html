Research

Generative AI for images, representations, and prediction.

I develop methods at the intersection of generative modeling, computer vision, imaging, and predictive AI. My work emphasizes faithful reconstruction, useful representation learning, and careful quantitative evaluation.

01 / Featured

RA-CMF

Region-Adaptive Conditional MeanFlow for CT Image Reconstruction. RA-CMF combines a Conditional MeanFlow reconstruction backbone with region-adaptive refinement so the system can apply additional correction where it is most useful while maintaining global image fidelity.
